The brief

A studio that designs space, in need of a brand that holds it.

Who is VAULTA? A modern architecture firm focused on innovative, sustainable and aesthetic design. They create functional spaces that blend creativity with precision, and they wanted an identity that felt exactly that way: modern, sophisticated and unmistakably architectural.

The challenge was to make the name do the work. VAULTA had to read as a studio of substance at a glance, stay minimal enough to live on a tiny favicon, and still feel premium blown up across a building site. One mark, many surfaces, zero compromise.

The idea

The name, drawn as a skyline.

Monogram

A vault that doubles as a cityscape.

We started by pulling apart the letterforms in "Vaulta" and rebuilding them as structure. The vertical and angular shapes inside the curve read as buildings, a small skyline, while the curve itself nods to a vault, one of the strongest forms in architecture. Minimal, geometric and instantly connected to the industry.

The result works as a full lockup, a standalone symbol, and a single mark small enough for an app icon, without ever losing its meaning.

The system

One symbol, built to flex.

The VAULTA symbol is the core of the brand. It ships in full colour, greyscale and standalone formats, with dedicated versions tuned for dark and light backgrounds so it always sits crisp on whatever it lands on.

Horizontal and vertical lockups give the team a confident option for every ratio, from a wide website header to a square social avatar.

The palette

Amber on black. Warm idea, serious frame.

Amber carries innovation and warmth, the spark of a bright idea. Black grounds it in strength and professionalism, the premium weight the studio wanted. Grey keeps things neutral and modern, and white holds the whole system clean and open.

Typography

Thin, wide and quietly futuristic.

A clean geometric sans, set light with generous tracking, gives the wordmark a modern, almost architectural line. It feels engineered rather than decorated, and leaves plenty of room for the mark to breathe.

The same type logic scales from the logo down to captions, keeping every layout calm, precise and unmistakably VAULTA.
